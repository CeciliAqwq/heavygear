# Heavy Gear — short English description

**Netherite, but make it hurt.** Heavy Gear turns falling into a weapon: upgrade netherite gear with a
Heavy Core and you get weapons and armour that smash exactly like a mace. Drop onto a creature and every
heavy armour piece adds **25%** of a same-height mace smash; heavy swords, axes, spears and arrows carry
the same smash, with vanilla's fall curve, Density, Breach and smash sound intact.

- **10 forgeable pieces** — heavy sword/axe/pickaxe/shovel/hoe, heavy spear, and a full set of heavy
  armour (each piece adds another 25% of the smash)
- **Ranged** — heavy bow, heavy crossbow and heavy arrows that smash on their way down (25%)
- **Heavy fishing rod** — the bobber smashes whatever it hooks; Wind Burst throws the catch into the air
- **Spear of Destiny** — heavy spear + trident, two modes on a hotkey, throwable
- **Potion of Heaviness** — for 3 minutes every weapon behaves heavy and every landing counts as a full
  armour set (add redstone for 8 minutes)
- **Compressed wind charges** — ×5 knockback with no fall damage, and the double version launches you
  65 blocks up
- **Dive Bombing** enchantment, 8 advancements, and the ecaM: the reverse mace that hurts *you*

Heavy Upgrade templates come from ominous vaults (≈16.7%), Trident Upgrade templates from elder
guardians (50%) and end city chests (10%).
